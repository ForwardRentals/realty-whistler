Before you buy

What makes Whistler different.

Whistler has rules most resort towns don't. A local agent and lawyer will walk you through them; these are the big ones to ask about.

  • Zoning and nightly rentalsOnly some properties are zoned for short-term nightly rentals. Check the zoning before you count on rental income.
  • Resident-restricted housingSome homes are restricted to people who live and work in Whistler. Ask whether a property has a covenant.
  • Strata and phased rulesMany Whistler condos have rental pools or use restrictions written into their strata or covenants.
  • Winter access and snow loadDriveways, parking and roofs work hard in winter. Ask about snow clearing and roof history.
  • Getting hereWhistler is about two hours from Vancouver on Highway 99, and less than an hour from Squamish.
